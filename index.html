Overnight, in two lines

A failure in air traffic control communications led the FAA to halt every departure in the US for about four and a half hours on Friday. It was the longest nationwide ground stop since the September 11 attacks. Nearly 10,000 flights were canceled. Officials blamed overloaded legacy switches and routers and said there is "zero evidence" so far of a cyberattack. Recovery could take days.

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet with Amex, Mastercard and Visa, and Sierra shipped version 1.0 of its agent-access protocol, which Shopify switched on for every store. Dario Amodei pitched Anthropic's IPO alongside Jamie Dimon ahead of a planned Thursday debut. Chip stocks fell again, with AMD down 8.8%, and three Fed presidents backed further rate hikes. Russia and the US put Europe's "security architecture" on the agenda for talks, and European officials pushed back.

Aviation · Infrastructure

US airspace shuts for four hours after air traffic control networks overload. It was the longest nationwide stop since 9/11

FAA · Reuters · Aviation Week · Washington Post · Nov 13
Began 3:36 pm ETReopened 8:15 pm ET~10,000 cancellationsAirline index −1.4%

What happened. The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop for all US airports on Friday afternoon, citing "communication issues impacting multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers." Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said the instability started at 3:36 pm Eastern (17:36 ART). "Our switches, our routers actually got overloaded with data and they started to give us inconsistencies," he said. He added that controllers never fully lost radar or radio contact and landed thousands of planes already in the air. Airspace reopened at 8:15 pm Eastern (22:15 ART), and departures are restarting gradually. By 11:30 pm, Flightradar24 counted nearly 10,000 canceled flights, including more than 2,200 each at Delta and American and 1,800 at United. Some aircraft waited more than five hours for a gate. Two airline officials told Reuters the failure appeared to affect a telecoms network run by L3Harris that carries controller–pilot radio frequencies. L3Harris did not respond to Reuters.

Cause and accountability. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was "zero evidence" so far of a cyberattack or a rogue AI system, but investigators have not ruled either out, and the NSA is assisting. "It appears this was mechanically driven," he said. Duffy blamed Congress for not funding a replacement system and asked for $50 billion to modernize air traffic control. Earlier on Friday, before the outage, the White House and the Transportation Department had asked Congress for $30.1 billion for airports and air traffic control. Reuters notes that airlines are not required to compensate stranded passengers because the failure was the FAA's, not theirs.

Why it matters (analysis)This is the most visible sign so far of strain on a system Reuters describes as running on aging equipment and short several thousand controllers. The near-term costs fall on airlines and travelers over the weekend before Thanksgiving, and air cargo that moves in passenger-plane bellies will be disrupted too. The political question is whether a failure this large moves modernization funding, which has stalled for years. Officials have not finished the investigation, so the stated cause is preliminary.

Agents · Payments · Commerce

The rails for agent shopping arrive on the same day: ChatGPT Wallet with the card networks, and Sierra's Poppy 1.0 on every Shopify store

OpenAI · The Next Web · Sierra · Becerra Bursátil · Nov 13
Amex · Mastercard · VisaUS Pro users firstPoppy: 100+ companiesV, MA +1.3%

What OpenAI launched. ChatGPT Wallet lets an agent pay for a user without seeing the actual card number. The user adds a card once, reviews the items, total and card before each purchase, and approves it with Face ID or a fingerprint. The network then issues a payment token, a substitute card number, so the merchant charges the user's card with its existing checkout. OpenAI says users keep card rewards and dispute protections and that merchants keep pricing, fulfillment, returns and the customer relationship. It is rolling out first through OpenAI's "dots" to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers. OpenAI says "over time" users will be able to pre-authorize purchases within limits such as spending caps or categories. It also published a merchant playbook: sites should recognize signed agent requests (Web Bot Auth) so firewalls don't block them, and work with risk providers so legitimate agent orders aren't declined. Named partners include Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd for fraud, and Adyen, Checkout.com and Stripe for payments.

What Sierra shipped. Sierra released version 1.0 of its Personal Agent Protocol ("Poppy"), a standard that lets a company tell a visiting agent who it represents and what it is allowed to do, such as check an order or book a flight. Per The Next Web, Shopify turned it on for every store, Meta's Muse agent (which Meta says more than 10 million people have set up) now looks for it first on every site, and more than 100 companies have signed up, including Delta, Intuit, Salesforce and Zoom. Mercury lets agents check balances and start payments under the account's approval rules, and Turkish Airlines lets them find fares and upgrades. The specification is still labeled a draft and allows breaking changes.

Why it matters (analysis)The two launches cover different layers: payment authorization (who pays, with what credential) and access (what an agent may do once it arrives). Both lean on existing systems instead of replacing them, which lowers the cost for merchants to say yes. Open questions remain: competing approaches from Visa, Mastercard and Amazon, which wants shoppers to use its own agent; how often fraud systems will wrongly decline agent orders; and who carries liability when a delegated purchase goes wrong. Adoption figures here come from the companies themselves.

Amodei pitches Anthropic's IPO with Dimon and names compute as his "deepest business dilemma"

$70–78 range825M sharesPrices Wed · trades ThuTicker ANT

In a livestreamed fireside chat with JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon, Amodei said Anthropic is "not super fussed" about exactly when or into what market conditions it lists, and repeated a line he said came from advisers: "never let your company be worth a single dollar more than it's really worth." Business Insider reported that he called the uncertainty about how much computing power to buy the company's "deepest business dilemma." The prospectus discloses about $900 billion of infrastructure commitments, around half due by 2030. He said the company buys "relatively little" compute in advance and counts on its gross margins and its cash (more than $95 billion plus $36 billion in short-term investments as of September 30, per the filing) to buy on the spot market if demand runs ahead. The offering is set at $70–78 a share for 825 million shares (CNBC), targeting about $1.4 trillion fully diluted (Business Insider). Pricing is planned for Wednesday, with Nasdaq trading on Thursday. JPMorgan leads domestic retail allocation, and Goldman Sachs is lead-left (Bloomberg). Analysis: the compute answer is the key one for investors. Commitments of that size against an uncertain revenue curve are the main bear argument, and the company's defense rests on margins and its cash pile.

PPI +0.3% m/m+6.5% y/yTruck freight +3.9%Food +1.3%

The October producer price index rose 0.3%, below the 0.4% economists expected, but the annual rate of 6.5% was the highest since November 2022 (BLS). Goods rose 0.5% and services 0.2%. Food rose 1.3% while energy slipped 0.1%. Core PPI, excluding food and energy, was +0.3% for the month and +5.0% for the year, matching forecasts (Barchart). For shippers, the notable line was truck freight transportation, up 3.9% in a single month (BLS, AJOT). Analysis: a monthly jump of that size in trucking costs lands during peak season and passes through to landed costs and delivery pricing. Whether it lasts depends on diesel and capacity, which this report doesn't explain.

Merval +2.9%Riesgo país 830Mayorista $1,543.50CCL gap 8.5%

Markets. The Merval rose 2.93% in pesos (Infobae's agency report says 2.68%, to 84,704), led by Ternium, Cresud and Central Puerto. Cabinet chief Diego Santilli asked the Senate constitutional affairs committee to seek a committee report. Ruling-party senators are skeptical the votes are there and see the move as pressure on governors. Neuquén has called provincial elections for February 14, 2027, and Tucumán for May 9. The presidential election is scheduled for October 24, 2027.

Europe gets a seat at Ukraine talks, but Moscow wants to discuss Europe's whole security order. Kyiv launches about 1,200 drones

Iran says it hit a US radar in Bahrain. CENTCOM says it shot down two drones and nothing was damaged

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they targeted a US early-warning radar in Bahrain in retaliation for what they described as an air attack on a telecoms tower in Sirik county, Hormozgan.
